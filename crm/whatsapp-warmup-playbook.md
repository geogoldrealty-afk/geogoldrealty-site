# WhatsApp Warm-Up Playbook

This is the first operating system for John and Angelo. The goal is to create conversations from people they already know.

## Daily Target

- John sends 50 warm WhatsApp or SMS messages.
- Angelo sends 50 warm WhatsApp or SMS messages.
- Total target is 100 touches per day.
- Start with a 10-contact dry run before the first full day.

## What Codex Automates

- Cleans the contact list.
- Creates the daily queue.
- Drafts the first message.
- Drafts the follow-up message.
- Creates one-click WhatsApp Web links.
- Creates one-click SMS links.
- Creates follow-up dates.
- Tracks who owns the next action.

## What Stays Human

- John or Angelo reviews the message.
- John or Angelo presses send.
- John or Angelo answers replies.
- John decides whether a reply is a real opportunity.

Do not auto-send WhatsApp messages in v1. The risk is not worth it. The speed gain is small compared with the risk of burning the phone number or sending the wrong message to a warm relationship.

## Morning Workflow

1. Open `crm/daily-outreach-generated.csv`.
2. Filter `sent_by` to your name.
3. Open the `wa_me_link` or `sms_link`.
4. Read the draft.
5. Personalize one sentence if needed.
6. Press send.
7. Change status from `queued` to `sent`.

## Reply Handling

Use these statuses:

- `replied`: They answered but there is no next step yet.
- `meeting booked`: A call or meeting is scheduled.
- `opportunity`: There is a real buyer, seller, investor, construction, mortgage, insurance, or referral angle.
- `not interested`: They answered no.
- `do not contact`: They asked not to be contacted again.

## Message Style

Keep it short. Do not sound like a newsletter.

Good first message:

```text
Hey Mike, it's John Scardino. Quick check-in. Do you or someone close to you need help buying, selling, flipping, or making sense of a property this year?
```

Good contractor message:

```text
Hey Anthony, it's John Scardino. Quick check-in. Are you seeing any homeowners, investors, or builders who need help buying, selling, flipping, or figuring out a property this month?
```

Good attorney/referral message:

```text
Hey Sarah, it's John Scardino. Quick check-in. If any clients need practical real estate help with a sale, purchase, property issue, or investor conversation, I'm taking those calls now.
```

## Seven-Day Success Criteria

- 700 total touches attempted.
- At least 50 replies.
- At least 10 real conversations.
- At least 3 booked calls.
- At least 1 serious opportunity.

If those numbers are weak, fix contact quality and message angle before adding paid ads.

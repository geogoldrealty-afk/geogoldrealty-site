Website inquiry privacy notice

Updated October 16, 2026. This notice describes the information you submit through the contact, seller, and property inquiry forms on geogoldrealty.com.

Information you provide

Depending on the form, you may provide your name, email address, telephone number, property address, contact preference, property details, message, and permission to contact you. A property inquiry may also include the address and MLS number of the home you selected.

How your inquiry is used

Geo Gold Realty uses this information to respond to your request, discuss your real estate needs, and manage follow-up. Please do not include Social Security numbers, banking details, passwords, or other sensitive documents in a website message.

Where your request goes

The website sends inquiries through its Vercel-hosted server to Geo Gold Realty’s Google Apps Script and Google Sheets CRM. Inquiry notifications are sent to Geo Gold Realty’s email inbox. These services process the information needed to deliver and store your request.

Other website services

Property searches use Garden State MLS data. Maps, fonts, and some images may be delivered by third-party services, which can receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads them. If you open an external virtual tour or another website, that service’s own privacy practices apply.

Your contact preferences

You can ask Geo Gold Realty to stop contacting you, correct your information, or review a request about your submitted information by emailing geogoldrealty@gmail.com or calling (201) 826-5730.
